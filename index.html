      Straight Answers

      Questions every parent and student asks

      Costs, timelines and the public versus private decision, explained plainly.

      We follow a fixed, written fee structure shared before you sign anything, split into clear milestones for counselling, admissions and visa filing. There are no hidden or surprise charges. University application fees, embassy fees and blocked account or tuition payments are paid directly by you to those institutions, and we show you each amount upfront.
      Plan on 4 to 8 months from profile evaluation to flying out. Admissions typically take 4 to 10 weeks, and visa processing varies by country: Germany often takes 6 to 12 weeks, the UK around 3 weeks after CAS, Canada 8 to 14 weeks and Australia 4 to 8 weeks. These are indicative timelines and embassy processing times can change, so start early.
      Public universities in Germany charge no tuition in most states, only a semester contribution, and they hold the same global recognition as their private peers. In the UK, Canada and Australia, public universities still charge international tuition, but usually less than private institutions and often with more scholarship options. We focus on public universities to protect both your budget and your visa case.
      Tuition is free at most German public universities, but you still need to show living funds through a blocked account (roughly €11,900 per year, so check the current figure), plus health insurance and a semester contribution. Some states or programs may charge fees, so we verify each university before you apply.
      You have options. We can enrol you in our IELTS band-guarantee program, target universities with lower minimums, or use a pre-sessional or foundation pathway where available. We never submit weak applications just to collect fees.
      No honest consultant can, because visa decisions are made solely by embassies. What we do is remove avoidable refusal risks through strong documentation, financial structuring, SOP consistency and mock interviews, which is why our clients enjoy a high approval rate.
