Apex Visa & Education Consultants

A premium, conversion-focused single-page website for a study abroad and IELTS academy targeting students in Lahore and Islamabad. Built with plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no frameworks, no build step and no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

The design follows a dark deep-navy SaaS aesthetic (glowing borders, sharp typography, electric blue and gold accents) and is fully responsive from budget phones to 4K displays.

Features
Sticky navigation with blur-on-scroll, mobile menu and a "Book Free Consultation" button
Hero section that addresses the visa-rejection fear, with a live-style readiness snapshot card
Animated stats grid (98% success rate, 5000+ students, 200+ universities, 0% hidden charges)
Services grid with hover elevation and a highlighted band-guarantee badge
3-step visa eligibility wizard (qualification, IELTS band, destination) with:
animated analyzing state and score ring
verdict badge: High Success Chance, Medium: Improvement Needed, or Needs Preparation
tailored action plan and matching scholarships per country and study level
"Get Detailed Blueprint Pack" lead form with validation
Testimonials grid with verified badges and result breakdowns
FAQ accordion with smooth collapse and ARIA support
Apex Smart Assistant, a floating chatbot with keyword routing, typing indicator, quick-action chips and auto-scroll
